## Top-Line Arguments

### For Americans
1. **Iran has 4.6x more oil than all 50 states combined** and the 2nd largest gas reserves on earth
2. **Controls the Strait of Hormuz** -- 27% of the world's seaborne oil. As a state, this is under US sovereignty
3. **Iranian-Americans are the most educated immigrant group in the US** -- a preview of what 90 million Iranians contribute
4. **Solves the nuclear problem permanently** -- no treaty needed when you're the same country
5. **Strategic position** between Russia, Central Asia, and the Middle East -- unbroken US corridor from NATO to the Indian Ocean
6. **$27.3 trillion in mineral resources** including the world's largest zinc and second-largest copper deposits
7. **Saves $20-50 billion per year** in military spending -- the US maintains 40,000+ troops on Gulf bases just to contain Iran. As a state, the Strait of Hormuz is a domestic waterway

### For Iranians
1. **There is a legal path** -- Texas did it in 1845, Hawaii did it in 1898. The Constitution allows it.
2. **The regime is collapsing** -- Khamenei is dead, proxies destroyed, economy in freefall. A real alternative is needed.
3. **89% of Iranians want democracy** -- American democracy is the most tested and proven model
4. **Full citizenship** -- constitutional rights, voting, freedom of speech, freedom of religion
5. **Economic liberation** -- sanctions gone overnight, access to global markets, investment, technology
6. **Brain drain reversed** -- instead of fleeing, the best and brightest build Iran within America
