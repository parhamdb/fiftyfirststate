# Iranian Religious Demographics: GAMAAN 2020 Survey

## The Gap Between Official Figures and Reality

Iran's government claims 99.98% of Iranians are Muslim. Anonymous surveys tell a radically different story.

## GAMAAN 2020 Survey (50,000+ respondents)

Conducted by the Group for Analyzing and Measuring Attitudes in Iran, affiliated with Tilburg University (Netherlands). Published in the Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion (peer-reviewed).

**Total identifying as Muslim: ~40%**
**Total non-Muslim / non-religious: ~60%**

## Key Findings

- 47% reported losing their religion during their lifetime
- Over 60% do not perform daily Muslim prayers
- 72% opposed compulsory hijab
- 70% supported separation of religion and politics
- No more than 9% strongly favored an Islamic political system

## Corroborating Evidence

- **Iran's own government poll (2020):** 60% of Iranians did not observe Ramadan fasting
- **Mosque closures:** 50,000 of 75,000 mosques closed due to declining attendance. Iran has the lowest mosque attendance of any Islamic country.
- **Pre-revolution comparison:** In 1975, over 80% always prayed and observed Ramadan. The theocracy paradoxically drove secularization.

## Why Anonymous Surveys Matter

In a country where apostasy is punishable by death, face-to-face surveys (like the World Values Survey showing 96.6% Muslim) produce unreliable results. People will not honestly report leaving Islam when it carries a death sentence. Anonymous online surveys are the only methodologically sound approach.

## Implications for Statehood

- Iran is NOT a Muslim-majority country by actual belief — it's roughly 40% Muslim
- Adding Iran as a state would NOT dramatically shift America's religious demographics
- Iran's population is highly secular — 70% want separation of religion and politics
- The theocratic government does NOT represent the religious views of its people
