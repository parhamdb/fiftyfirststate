## Branding Recommendations

### Primary Domain: iran51.com
- .com is the strongest TLD for reach and credibility
- iran51.us as secondary -- reinforces American statehood angle

### Defensive Registrations
Consider registering to prevent squatters:
- iran51.org, 51iran.com, iran51st.com

### Social Media: @iran51 Everywhere
- Short, clean, identical across platforms
- Easy to type, remember, print on materials
- Fallback: @iran51st if @iran51 is taken on any platform

### SEO Opportunity
- "Iran 51st state" and "Iran 51" are not competitive search terms -- **own this niche immediately**
- iran51.org would rank quickly with zero competition
- The US-Iran conflict creates massive search interest in Iran-related topics -- opportunity to capture traffic

### Hashtag Strategy
- Primary: **#Iran51**
- Secondary: #51stState, #IranStatehood
- Narrative: #OneNationTwoHomelands, #IranAmericaTogether
- Community: #IranianAmerican, #IranianDiaspora, #Tehrangeles
